## The Problem

Getting a document printed on campus shouldn't be complicated.

Students often rely on WhatsApp messages, manual communication, and repeated visits to Xerox shops just to get their documents printed. During busy college hours, this creates unnecessary waiting, confusion, and wasted time.

**A simple everyday task becomes an inconvenient process.**

## The Solution

Sprinto simplifies campus printing by bringing the ordering process online.

📄 **Upload Documents**  
Send documents for printing without depending on scattered chat messages.

⚙️ **Choose Print Preferences**  
Specify your printing requirements while placing an order.

💳 **Simplify Checkout**  
Make the ordering process more convenient with digital payment support.

📦 **Know Where to Collect**  
Get clear collection information so you can plan when to pick up your prints.

## Why Sprinto?

Less confusion. Less unnecessary coordination. A better printing experience for students.

Sprinto connects the student ordering experience with an organized printing workflow, helping make campus Xerox services simpler and more convenient.

## Our Mission

We believe students should spend less time managing print orders and more time focusing on what matters.

**Sprinto is built to make campus printing simple.**
